Privacy Policy

Learn how CavoTV handles media, account connections, and playback information, and how you can manage that data.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Three things to know. CavoTV is a media playback and management tool and does not supply movies, shows, or channels. Media catalogs, favorites, and playback progress are mainly stored on your device. When you choose to connect to subscriptions, media servers, websites, or receivers, those services receive the information needed to fulfill your requests.

01Scope

This policy describes how the CavoTV app handles information when you use live TV subscriptions, local videos, private media libraries, program guides, AirPlay or Chromecast casting, CarPlay, and web playback.

The app does not require a separate CavoTV account. Accounts for private media libraries and subscriptions belong to the third-party services you choose. Third-party websites, media servers, system services, and the platform hosting this page describe their data practices in their own policies.

03Permissions are your choice

  • Local network: Used to discover compatible receivers, connect to local media servers, and transfer video where needed. Denying access may affect these features, but does not prevent local playback that requires no network connection.
  • Camera: Requested only when you use QR code scanning, to identify a playlist URL you provide. Scanning is not used to take photos or upload camera images.
  • File selection: The system file picker provides access to the files you select. Imports are copied into the app directory and may require additional storage.

You can change granted permissions in iOS Settings or choose to enter an address manually. Revoking a permission does not automatically delete previously imported content or records. See the retention and deletion section below.

04Third parties, casting, and network transfers

Services you choose to connect to

Live sources, program guide services, artwork services, and media servers receive the information needed to establish a network connection. Authentication information is sent to the server address you enter. Some services use their official authorization pages for sign-in, and requests or playback URLs may carry account credentials as required by the protocol. Use only sources you trust and are authorized to access.

Compatible devices and platform services

Chromecast uses the Google Cast SDK for device discovery, connection, and playback control. AirPlay and CarPlay use Apple system capabilities. Receivers and their platforms may process device, connection, and playback information under their own rules. See the Google Privacy Policy, the Apple Privacy Policy, and the privacy policies of the media services you connect to.

Temporary local network streaming

When temporary streaming is needed, the app first prompts you to use trusted Wi-Fi, creates a random temporary access URL, and revokes it when the relevant session ends. HTTP does not encrypt the connection. A random URL alone cannot eliminate risks on the same network. Choose HTTPS sources and avoid transferring private content over untrusted public networks.

Web browsing and international connections

The in-car browser uses a temporary WebKit data store, but website servers can still record visits. Temporary browsing is not anonymous browsing. Media servers, account authorization services, search services, and websites you choose may be located in other countries or regions. Data destinations depend on the services you actually connect to. This policy makes no promise about those third parties’ storage locations or retention periods.

05Retention and deletion

  • Local videos: Deleting a video in the app removes the copy stored by the app. It does not delete the original file or a server file.
  • Subscriptions and servers: Removing a source can clear its local catalog and associated records. Removing a media library connection clears the corresponding login credentials stored by the app. To revoke third-party authorization, also use that service’s account management page.
  • Favorites and progress: These support content management and resume playback. Use the app’s management controls or remove the associated source. Turning off a feature does not necessarily erase all of its historical data.
  • Uninstalling: This generally removes local data in the app sandbox. System backups and Keychain information may have different lifecycles. Do not rely on uninstalling alone to revoke third-party account authorization.
  • Support materials: Information you submit should be retained only as long as needed to handle your request and any necessary follow-up review, subject to applicable retention requirements. Further details will be provided when the relevant channels become available.

Before deleting a source or uninstalling the app, back up local content that cannot be recovered elsewhere. Device and iCloud backups depend on your system settings and do not mean that CavoTV provides cloud synchronization.

06Your choices and rights

You can choose not to add network sources, connect third-party accounts, enable casting, or use scanning, and you can stop using these features at any time. You can view, change, or delete locally stored content using the app’s management features. We cannot remotely read the media library on your device.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of, restriction of processing of, or other action concerning personal information we actually hold, or withdraw consent. We will verify requests only to the extent necessary and reasonable and respond within applicable time limits. Requests concerning third-party servers should be directed to the relevant provider. Do not voluntarily send passwords or complete access tokens for identity verification.

07Use by minors

Minors should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian and access age-appropriate, lawfully authorized content. CavoTV does not provide a dedicated children’s library or guarantee age ratings for user-added sources. If a parent or guardian believes a feedback channel has received a child’s personal information, they may request action through the officially published privacy contact channel.

08Policy updates

This policy may change as features and data practices evolve. The published version will state its effective date. Material changes to processing purposes, information types, or sharing practices will be communicated appropriately, and renewed authorization will be sought where required by law. A new policy does not replace any consent that must be obtained under applicable law.
