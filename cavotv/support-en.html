Frequently asked questions

How do I add channels or local videos?

In Add Content, select a subscription URL, configuration file, local video, or private media library as appropriate. Use playlist sources you are authorized to access. Local configuration files may use UTF-8-encoded M3U, M3U8, or TXT formats. Each configuration file should be no larger than 5 MB.

Local videos are copied into the app directory, so sufficient storage is required. CavoTV does not include channels, provide subscription URLs, or sell media accounts.

Why won’t imported media play, or why is there only audio?
  1. Check that the original file or source URL is still valid, your account has not expired, and the required network is reachable.
  2. Try another file or channel known to work to determine whether the issue affects one source or all content.
  3. If a channel has multiple playback sources, switch manually. Recovery behavior may differ between a failure to start and an interruption after playback has begun.
  4. Note what happens to the picture and sound and any error message. Do not post complete playback URLs containing passwords or tokens in public.

Codec support varies by device. Playback on a phone does not guarantee playback on a TV. The app does not guarantee automatic transcoding or playback of DRM-protected content.

Why can’t I connect to a private media library or subscription service?

Check the server address, retain any required reverse proxy path, and verify account permissions, certificates, ports, and local network access. Sign in using the appropriate server account, the provider’s official authorization page, or the connection details supplied by the provider.

If authorization has expired, authorize the connection again. HTTP is not encrypted; prefer correctly configured HTTPS. App support cannot reset third-party passwords, activate accounts, or restore content removed by another provider.

Why can’t I find my TV, or see video when using AirPlay with a Mac?
  1. Connect your phone and receiver to a network where they can reach each other. Guest Wi-Fi, client isolation, and some VPNs may prevent discovery.
  2. Check CavoTV’s Local Network permission in iOS Settings and confirm that the relevant receiving feature is enabled on the other device.
  3. Connect to AirPlay through the system device picker, or search for Chromecast devices within the app. A Mac must support AirPlay receiving, have it enabled, and allow the sending device in its receiver settings.
  4. Start playback on your phone, then select the receiver. Try an ordinary video format supported by the receiver to distinguish format problems from network problems.
  5. If your phone needs to provide a temporary stream, keep the app in the foreground and avoid locking the screen or changing networks.

In your report, include the receiver model, operating system version, and the specific behavior, such as no response, audio only, or a black screen.

Why does CarPlay only show audio, or not display video?

Standard CarPlay can provide media browsing and audio controls. Video and web capabilities also depend on the iOS version, the connected in-car display setup, the vehicle, and system restrictions. Meeting the operating system version requirement does not guarantee vehicle video support. Refer to the actual connection status and available features.

Check the connection only while safely parked and when permitted by the system. Do not watch video or browse websites while driving, and do not attempt to bypass vehicle safety restrictions.

Why won’t a web video open or cast?

First confirm that the website is accessible. Websites may restrict external playback, embedded browsers, or access from certain regions. Sign-in requirements, paid content, and DRM can also affect playback. Try the website’s official app or browser interface and follow its service rules. A web entry point does not imply a partnership between CavoTV and the website or guarantee compatibility.

Why is the program guide missing or mismatched?

Program guides come from the configured XMLTV URL or media service. Check that the guide source is reachable, channel identifiers match, and your device’s time and time zone are correct, then try refreshing. The app cannot supply missing program data if the source does not provide it.

What happens when I delete a video, remove a connection, or uninstall?

Deleting a local video removes only the copy stored in the app. Removing a media library connection clears the relevant local connection information and records, but does not delete media on the server. Back up important content before uninstalling. Also use the third-party service’s account management controls to revoke authorization. Device backups and system secure storage may retain information, so uninstalling alone should not be relied on to revoke authorization.
